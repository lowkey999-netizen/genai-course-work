How High-Dimensional Embeddings Actually Work

No brain-melting 768D geometry required: a dimension is just a slider.

1 A Dimension is Just a Trait (A Slider)

In physical space, 3D means Left/Right, Up/Down, and Forward/Back. But in computer science, a dimension is simply any property you can measure with a number.

Example: Describing a Real House
• Bedrooms: 3
• Square Feet: 1800
• Distance to School: 1.5 km
• Price in Lakhs: 85
Vector: [3, 1800, 1.5, 85] (A 4D Point!)

To an AI model, human language works the same way! Instead of 4 house traits, the AI has 768 abstract sliders representing semantic properties.
